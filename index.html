See it work

A short look at Ironbridge

A quick walk through the system, from a new load to a paid invoice.

The driver app

Sign in, fuel on the route, mark delivered, scan the POD, truck papers and settlements, all from the driver's phone.

What it does

The daily work of a small carrier, in one place

Every carrier does the same loop. Ironbridge keeps the whole loop in one system, so nothing is retyped between a spreadsheet, a billing tool and a pay sheet.

Dispatch

Loads and the load board

Enter a load or scan the rate confirmation and the fields fill in for you. Assign the driver, truck and trailer, and watch each load move from booked to delivered.

Get paid

Invoices and receivables

Delivered loads become invoices. See what is open, what is overdue and what has been paid, and click any total to see the invoices behind it.

Pay drivers

Settlements

Add up a driver's loads for the pay period, apply your own deductions and advances, and print a clear statement. Company drivers and owner-operators are both handled.

Fleet

Drivers, trucks and trailers

One record for each person and piece of equipment, with an address book of the brokers, shippers and receivers you work with.

Your team

Users and permissions

Give each person their own sign-in and choose what they can see or change. Administrators sign in with a second step for extra protection.

Help

Built-in help

A Help page inside the app explains each part of the system, and shows only the topics for the modules your company uses.

Modules

Add only what your company needs

The core system handles loads, invoices, settlements and fleet. Modules switch on extra pages and features, and each one is priced on its own.

01

Fuel cards and pricing

Import card transactions, check them against the fuel bill, and keep daily pricing in front of dispatch.

02

ELD and fuel level

Connect your ELD for tank level, MPG and odometer readings. Geotab is supported today.

03

IFTA and mileage

Miles and fuel by state for your quarterly return, from an upload, from your ELD, or entered by hand.

04

QuickBooks

Post invoices, bills and settlements to your own QuickBooks Online account.

05

Inbox and email reading

Forward rate confirmations and receipts to your company's address and they land ready to review.

06

Costs and management

Profit per load, overhead, staff and per-mile costs, so you can see what a load really earned.

07

Compliance

Expiration dates for driver and equipment documents, plus a random testing pool with a record of each draw.

Who builds it

Built by a carrier that uses it

Ironbridge started inside a working dry van and reefer carrier. The software was written to run that company's loads, billing and driver pay, so it was shaped by real dispatch days and not by a feature list.

Your company's data stays separateEach company's records are stored apart from every other company's and are only available to the people you sign in.
Two-step sign-in for administratorsAdministrators confirm their sign-in with a code from an authenticator app.
Nothing is sent without youEmails and notices that go outside your company are off until you turn them on.
